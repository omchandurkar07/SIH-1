Irregular Water Supply in Village X

visibility 1.2k Views

Over the past three months, the primary water supply pipeline serving the eastern and southern sectors of Village X has experienced severe irregularities. Residents report that water pressure drops significantly during peak morning hours, and often, the supply stops entirely for periods exceeding 48 hours.

This disruption heavily impacts daily routines, agricultural micro-activities, and sanitation. Previous maintenance requests have noted potential blockages or pressure valve failures in the main reservoir line connecting to the village intake station. Immediate investigation and structural repair are required to restore reliable service.
